Questions

Does it work with the app closed?

Yes. iOS wakes Lest when you cross into the area. Location has to be set to Always; the app asks once and says why.

How does it know which Target?

It picks the closest one on Apple Maps. Wrong one? Tap the reminder and change it on the map; Lest remembers your choice.

What if it can’t find the time of something?

The reminder still saves and asks you to set the time with a picker. Nothing goes off at a guess.
