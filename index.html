What it does

Three kinds of reminder.
One way to set them all.

Place

Fires when you get there.
Or when you leave.

“Grab paper towels when I pass Costco.” “Take the bins out when I get home.” “Umbrella when I leave.” Lest resolves the place, draws a fence around it with iOS’s own region monitoring, and your phone wakes it when you cross the line. The app doesn’t have to be open. It doesn’t have to be today.

  • Arrive or leave, your choice of words
  • Home and work are remembered places
  • “The gym” means your gym, once you’ve said which

Event

Looks up the time
so you don’t have to.

“Record the next Texans game.” “Remind me about the Severance finale.” “The SpaceX launch.” Lest finds when it’s on, sets the reminder a little before it starts, and shows you the listing it used so you can check it.

  • NFL, NBA and MLB schedules, straight from the source
  • Shows, premieres and launches, looked up on the web
  • Wrong time? Change it with a picker, right on the card

Time

Plain time reminders too.
Just say when.

“Call the vet tomorrow at nine.” “In twenty minutes, check the oven.” “Every Monday at noon, the timesheet.” Set instantly, on your phone, no lookup needed. Anything without a time or place lands in Thoughts instead of nagging you.

  • Relative, absolute, and recurring times
  • Thoughts: names, plans, that restaurant, saved for later
  • Send one to a friend as a text, written out

How it works

What happens in the three seconds after you stop talking.

  1. 1

    Transcribed on your iPhone

    Speech recognition runs on the device. No audio is uploaded, ever.

  2. 2

    The sentence is read for a when and a where

    The words go to Lest’s server, which works out the trigger: a place, an event, a time, or none. Only the words, your time zone, and your city.

  3. 3

    The place or time is resolved

    Places through Apple Maps, near you. Games from league schedules. Shows and launches with a web lookup. Then the reminder is armed on your phone.

  4. 4

    It waits, and fires

    Region monitoring wakes the app when you arrive. Timed ones fire like any alarm. You get one notification, with the words you said.

Questions

Does it work with the app closed?

Yes. iOS region monitoring wakes Lest when you cross into the area, whether the app is open or not. Location has to be set to Always for that; the app asks once and explains why.

How does it know which Target?

It searches Apple Maps near you and takes the closest one that matches. If it picked wrong, tap the reminder and change it on the map; Lest remembers your choice.

What if it can’t find the time of something?

The reminder still saves and asks you to set the time with a picker. Nothing is lost, and nothing goes off at a guessed time.

How accurate are the place reminders?

They fire when your phone reports you inside the area around the place, typically within a minute of arriving. Big stores and homes work best; a specific aisle is not something any phone can do.

Is there a free plan?

There’s a free first month with everything included. After that it’s a subscription through the App Store; cancel any time in Settings.
