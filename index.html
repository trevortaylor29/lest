How it works

  1. 1

    You say it

    Transcribed on your iPhone. No audio leaves the phone.

  2. 2

    Lest sets it

    It finds the when or the where in your sentence: the Target near you, the time of the game.

  3. 3

    It fires there

    When you arrive, or when the time comes. One notification, in your words.

Your voice and your location stay on your phone.

Speech is transcribed on the device and place reminders use iOS’s own region monitoring. No account, no sign-up. The privacy policy is short and says exactly what leaves the phone.

Questions

Does it work with the app closed?

Yes. iOS wakes Lest when you cross into the area. Location has to be set to Always; the app asks once and says why.

How does it know which Target?

It picks the closest one on Apple Maps. Wrong one? Tap the reminder and change it on the map; Lest remembers your choice.

What if it can’t find the time of something?

The reminder still saves and asks you to set the time with a picker. Nothing goes off at a guess.
