How it works

  1. 1

    You say it

    Speech becomes text on your iPhone. No audio ever leaves the phone.

  2. 2

    Lest sets it

    It reads your sentence for a when and a where, then finds the store near you or the time of the game.

  3. 3

    It comes back

    When you arrive, when you leave, or when the time comes. One notification, in your words.

Your voice and your location stay on your phone.

Place reminders use the same region monitoring iOS uses for everything else, and nothing about where you go is sent anywhere. No account, no sign-up. The privacy policy is short and says exactly what leaves the phone.

Questions

Does it work with the app closed?

Yes. iOS wakes Lest when you cross into the area. Location has to be set to Always. The app asks once and says why.

What if it can’t find the time of something?

The reminder still saves and asks you to set the time with a picker. Nothing is ever set at a guess.

What if there’s no when or where yet?

It waits in Thoughts. Say it the moment you stop talking and sort it out later.
