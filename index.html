Lest

Reminders, but when they matter. Say it once; Lest works out the when and the where.

Get help

Email trev@holliday.io. Replies come from a person, usually within a day.

Include your Support ID, which is in the app under Settings, Support. It is a random identifier, not an account, and it is the only way we can find what happened on your end. The "Email support" button in Settings fills it in for you.

Common questions

A place reminder never went off. Location has to be set to Always: iOS Settings, Lest, Location, Always. The reminder card says "Won't fire yet" until it is. Place reminders fire when you arrive (or, for "when I leave", when you go), and they need the phone to notice you crossing into the area, which can take a minute.

The time it looked up was wrong. Tap the reminder, then "Wrong time? Change it" to set it by hand, and tell us what you said so we can fix the lookup.

It picked the wrong store. Tap the reminder and "Change it" to pick the right one on the map. Lest remembers it for next time.

Subscriptions, trials and refunds. Your subscription is managed by Apple: iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Cancel any time in the trial and nothing is charged. Refunds are issued by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your data. Settings, "Reset everything" wipes the app's data on your phone. To have the small amount of server-side data tied to your Support ID removed, email us the ID.
