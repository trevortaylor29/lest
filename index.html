By time, location, or event

One sentence. Any kind of reminder.

When you get there.

"Grab paper towels when I pass Costco." "Take the bins out when I get home." "Umbrella when I leave." Place reminders fire on arrival, or on the way out, using your iPhone's own region monitoring.

When the game is on.

"Record the next Texans game." "Remind me about the Severance finale." Lest looks up when it's on and reminds you a little before it starts.

Which store? It remembers.

Say "the gym" once and pick it on the map. Every "the gym" after that is that gym. Wrong one? Change it on the map; that's remembered too.

Thoughts

Don't know when yet?

Names, plans, that restaurant Kyle mentioned. Say it and it lands in Thoughts instead of nagging you for a time. Sorted later, or never.

Privacy

Your voice stays on your phone.

On-device speech

iPhone transcribes what you say. Audio is never sent anywhere.

Location never leaves

Place reminders use iOS region monitoring on the device. Only your city is sent with a capture, never coordinates.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. Your reminders live in a database on your phone.

Questions

Does it work with the app closed?

Yes. Place reminders use iOS region monitoring, so your phone wakes Lest when you cross into the area. Location has to be set to Always for that; the app asks once and says why.

Plain time reminders too?

Of course. "Call the vet tomorrow at nine." "Every Monday at noon, submit the timesheet." Those are set instantly, no lookup needed.

What if it picks the wrong store?

Tap the reminder, tap "Change it", pick the right one on the map. Lest remembers it for next time.

What if it can't find the time of something?

The reminder still saves, and asks you to set the time with a picker. Nothing is lost.

Is there a free plan?

There's a free first month with everything included. After that it's a subscription through the App Store; cancel any time in Settings.
